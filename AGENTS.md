# Security reports

- **Reporters:** follow the format in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and assess applicability using [THREAT_MODEL.md](THREAT_MODEL.md). Write concise, factual reports; distinguish verified behavior from assumptions. Do not attach a patch; the GLPI team will offer you the opportunity to review its patch before publication.
- **Maintainers:** use [$glpi-security-triage](.agents/skills/glpi-security-triage/SKILL.md) to assess incoming reports and draft replies, and [$glpi-security-patch](.agents/skills/glpi-security-patch/SKILL.md) to reproduce bugs, fix them, and independently verify the result.

Read the selected skill for setup and detailed instructions. Always keep reports and unreleased fixes private.
